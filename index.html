    The problem

    Following up old quotes by hand takes hours, so it never happens. Leads you paid for go cold.

    What it does

    Sends personal emails in small daily batches, then reads and sorts every reply.

    How replies go

    Interested: your booking link. A question: answered from your approved facts. "Stop": never emailed again.

    How it stays safe

    Unclear or upset replies go to a person. Drafts that fail a check are held, never sent and never lost.

    You stay in control

    • Anything unclear or upset comes to you, not the AI.
    • It only says what you've approved. No made-up prices or promises.
    • Nobody gets emailed twice, or without permission.

    Leads from HighLevel, Jobber, HubSpot, or a simple spreadsheet. Emails from your own Gmail or Outlook.
